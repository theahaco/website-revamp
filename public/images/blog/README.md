# Images inside articles

Name each image after its article, with a number:
`what-a-bank-should-ask-1.webp`, `what-a-bank-should-ask-2.webp`.
Rename it on your computer, then click **Add file**, **Upload files**.

In the article, write this on its own line (the text in brackets describes
the image for people who cannot see it):

`![What the image shows](/images/blog/what-a-bank-should-ask-1.webp)`

At most 1600 px wide and 300 KB.
