# Case study thumbnails

> **Not shown at the moment.** Since November 2026 the Work page and the home
> page list their rows without images. These files are kept in case the lists get
> images again; nothing needs to be uploaded here for now.

The small image next to each case study on the Work page (the home page list
has no images). One image per case study, with exactly the same name as the
case study file: `lifi.md` uses `lifi.webp` (`.png` or `.jpg` also work).
There is nothing to write in the case study: the rows find the image by its
name.

What it shows: the diagram of the case study's header image, alone on its
background color. No title, logo or text (labels are unreadable this small).

Size: 400 x 400 px (square), under 30 KB, the diagram in the middle with space around
it: computers show a square cut from the middle, phones a wide strip.

No thumbnail yet? Nothing breaks: the row shows the header image
(`public/images/cases/<name>.webp`) by its right side, where its diagram is
(on phones, the whole header image). Add the thumbnail here when it is ready,
with **Add file > Upload files** on your branch, as in `docs/CONTENT.md`.

Clients under NDA: no name, logo or detail that identifies them, in the image
or its file name.
