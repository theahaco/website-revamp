# Article thumbnails

The small square image next to each article in the article lists: the rows
of the Work page's Articles view and of the home page's Articles tab, "More
articles" under each article, and the Writing list of the author's team page.
On the Work page and the home page, the newest article is shown larger, as a
tile, with its cover cut to its diagram instead. One image per article, with exactly the same name as the
article file: `what-a-bank-should-ask.md` uses `what-a-bank-should-ask.webp`
(`.png` or `.jpg` also work).

What it shows: the diagram of the article's cover, alone on its background
color, without title, logo or text. Size: 400 x 400 px (square), under 30 KB,
the diagram in the middle (the rows show the whole square).

No thumbnail yet? Nothing breaks: the row shows the cover
(`public/images/covers/<name>`) by its right side, where its diagram is.
Upload the thumbnail here when it is ready, as in `docs/CONTENT.md`.
