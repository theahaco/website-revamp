# Article thumbnails

> **Not shown at the moment.** Since November 2026 the Work page and the home
> page list their rows without images. These files are kept in case the lists get
> images again; nothing needs to be uploaded here for now.

The small image next to each article in the Articles list of the Work page
(the home page list has no images). One image per article, with exactly the
same name as the article file: `what-a-bank-should-ask.md` uses
`what-a-bank-should-ask.webp` (`.png` or `.jpg` also work).

What it shows: the diagram of the article's cover, alone on its background
color, without title, logo or text. Size: 400 x 400 px (square), under 30 KB, the
diagram in the middle (computers show a square cut from the middle, phones a
wide strip).

No thumbnail yet? Nothing breaks: the row shows the cover
(`public/images/covers/<name>`) by its right side, where its diagram is (on
phones, the whole cover). Upload the thumbnail here when it is ready, as in
`docs/CONTENT.md`.
