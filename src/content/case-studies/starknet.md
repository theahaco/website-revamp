sections:
- id: challenge
  title: Challenge
  body: 'Starknet is a Layer 2 network for Ethereum built on zero-knowledge proofs. Its onboarding has to work for hackathon participants and curious developers, who give a new network an evening, not a week. Going through it, our engineers found that even experienced engineers needed several working days to get functional code.'
- id: solution
  title: Solution
  body: |-
    Two of our engineers went through Starknet's onboarding documentation for a week, then wrote three documents for the Starknet team:

    - An analysis of the core onboarding journey, with feedback on the experience as a whole
    - Suggestions for the Starknet command line, small and large, given its central place in a developer's day
    - At the team's request, a short explanation of what Loam, the tool [Stellar Scaffold](/open-source/stellar-scaffold) grew out of, could look like on Starknet

    We then went through the audit with the team on a call, to agree next steps.
- id: outcome
  title: Outcome
  body: Shortly after our feedback, Starknet restructured the quickstart of its documentation, taking up some of our suggestions. The team decided Loam was not for them at that time.
