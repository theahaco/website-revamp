Fabricius has fifteen years of engineering experience, five of them building Web3 systems for institutions such as MoneyGram, the Central Bank of Indonesia and IOG. He holds Hyperledger Fabric and Besu certifications.

He designed Moonlight, our privacy layer for Stellar payments (protocol, core contracts and SDK), and is a core contributor to the Stellar Development Foundation's Architect Program, working on institutional proposals and on the largest integrations the Foundation has pushed on Stellar over the past three years.
