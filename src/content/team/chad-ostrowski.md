Chad moved into Web3 in 2019 after years in web startups and consulting, building Ethereum DAO applications and then working on NEAR Protocol. He has built The Aha Company's developer tooling since 2021.

On Stellar he wrote the contract client in the official JavaScript SDK (contract.Client and AssembledTransaction) and the Stellar CLI's command tree and TypeScript bindings generator. He also built the provider platform of Moonlight, our privacy layer for Stellar payments. On XRPL he built Carbon Coin, a multi-purpose token demo with a multisig issuer, freeze and clawback.
