Zach loves building tools people enjoy using. He's spent time as a teacher, engineer, and consultant most recently working at Hug where he led the UI team building a Web3 e-commerce platform connecting artists and curators. He joined The Aha Co in 2024 and has been building on Stellar ever since, currently maintaining [Stellar Scaffold](https://github.com/stellar-scaffold/) and the [Contract Explorer](https://github.com/theahaco/contract-explorer).

Closer to home, Zach sits on the board of [a community non-profit](https://techlancaster.org/) supporting local tech meetups. He regularly gives tech talks, runs workshops, and [occasionally writes](https://zachfedor.com/).
